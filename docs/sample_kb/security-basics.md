# Security basics for everyone

## Passwords
Use a password manager. Work passwords must be at least 14 characters and must not be reused anywhere else. IT will never ask for your password or MFA code - anyone who does is attempting a scam.

## Lost or stolen device
Report a lost or stolen laptop or phone to IT immediately, day or night, in #it-help or by emailing it-help@example.com. We can lock and remotely wipe company devices. Also file a police report for theft.

## Working in public
Lock your screen when you step away (Ctrl+Cmd+Q on Mac, Win+L on Windows). Avoid public Wi-Fi without the VPN for sensitive work, and use a privacy screen on trains and planes.

## Sharing files externally
Share files with external partners using expiring links from the company cloud drive, not personal email or USB drives.
