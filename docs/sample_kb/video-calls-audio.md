# Video calls: audio and camera problems

## Nobody can hear me
- Check you are not muted in the meeting app and that the headset mute switch is off.
- In the meeting app's audio settings, pick the correct microphone (the headset, not the laptop or monitor).
- On Mac, check System Settings > Privacy & Security > Microphone and allow the meeting app.

## Camera not working
- Close other apps that might be using the camera.
- Allow camera access in Privacy & Security settings, then restart the meeting app.
- External webcams: try a different USB port, preferably directly on the laptop rather than through a dock.

## Echo or feedback
Use a headset, or make sure only one device in the room has audio joined.
